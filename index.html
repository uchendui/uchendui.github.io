About

I am a doctoral student at Harvard University in the Edge Computing Lab, co-advised by Vijay Janapa Reddi and Kianté Brantley, and a Kempner Institute Graduate Fellow. Prior to this, I worked as an AI Resident at Google Brain.

Previously, I received a BS/MS in computer science from Michigan State University.

My main research interest is in how we can better use foundation models as agents in the real world that can improve over time with more experience.
